# Nimbus Cascade Inc. — Employee Handbook

This handbook describes the policies, benefits, and processes that apply to employees of
Nimbus Cascade Inc. It supersedes any prior handbook version. It does not apply to
contractors or vendors unless a section explicitly says otherwise.

## Company Overview

### Who We Are

Nimbus Cascade Inc. was founded in 2014 and is headquartered in Portland, with satellite
offices in Austin and Halifax. The company operates on a **hybrid-first** model: employees
are expected in an office **2 days per week** unless covered by the Remote Work Policy.

### Org Structure

The company is organized into four divisions: Platform Engineering, Customer Success,
Finance & Operations, and People & Talent. Each division is led by a Vice President who
reports to the Chief Operating Officer.

## Leave Policy

### Annual Leave

Full-time employees accrue **23 days** of annual leave per calendar year, credited at a
rate of 1.9166 days per completed month of service. Unused annual leave may be carried
over into the following year up to a maximum of **9 days**; anything beyond that is
forfeited on March 31st.

### Sick Leave

Employees receive **14 days** of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is
required for any sick leave taken for more than **2 consecutive days**. Unused sick leave
does not carry over and does not convert to a cash payout.

### Parental Leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to **20 weeks** of paid parental leave. Secondary
caregivers are entitled to **6 weeks**. Parental leave must be requested at least
**60 days** before the expected start date via the internal tool **LeaveHub**.

### Bereavement Leave

Employees receive **5 days** of paid bereavement leave for the loss of an immediate
family member, and **2 days** for an extended family member. Additional unpaid leave can
be arranged with a manager's approval.

### Jury Duty and Civic Leave

Nimbus Cascade pays full salary for up to **10 business days** of jury duty per year.
Employees must submit a copy of the jury summons to People & Talent via **LeaveHub**
within **3 days** of receiving it.

### Sabbatical Leave

Employees with **5 or more years** of continuous service are eligible for a **4-week**
paid sabbatical, taken in addition to annual leave. Sabbaticals must be scheduled at
least **90 days** in advance and are subject to team capacity approval.

## Reimbursement and Expense Policy

### Eligible Expenses

Reimbursable expenses include client travel, conference registration fees, and
work-related software subscriptions under **$85/month** without prior approval.
Subscriptions above that threshold require sign-off from a Director in **ExpenseHawk**.

### How to Submit a Claim

1. Log the expense in **ExpenseHawk** within **21 days** of the purchase date.
2. Attach an itemized receipt — credit card statements alone are not accepted.
3. Claims are routed to the employee's manager for approval, then to Finance.
4. Approved claims are paid out in the next bi-weekly payroll cycle, not immediately.

Claims submitted more than **45 days** after the purchase date are not eligible for
reimbursement except in cases of documented travel delays.

### Reimbursement Cap

The maximum single-claim amount that can be self-approved by a manager is **$1,240**.
Anything above that requires additional approval from the VP of Finance.

### Meals and Entertainment

Client meals are reimbursable up to **$95 per person** without prior approval. Team
social events are budgeted separately through the manager's discretionary fund and are
capped at **$40 per employee per quarter**.

## Travel Policy

### Booking Process

All flights and hotels must be booked through **TripNimbus**, the company's designated
travel portal. Bookings made outside TripNimbus are reimbursed at **70%** of cost unless
TripNimbus was unavailable at the time of booking.

### Class of Travel

Economy class is standard for flights under **6 hours**. Flights longer than **6 hours**
may be booked in premium economy. Business class requires VP approval regardless of
flight duration.

### International Travel

Employees traveling internationally for work must complete the **Travel Safety
Briefing** in LeaveHub at least **14 days** before departure and must carry the
company-issued travel insurance card, valid for trips up to **30 consecutive days**.

### Mileage Reimbursement

Personal vehicle use for work purposes is reimbursed at **$0.61 per mile**, logged
through ExpenseHawk's mileage tracker. Commutes to an employee's regular office are not
reimbursable.

## Remote Work Policy

### Eligibility

Roles classified as "hybrid-eligible" in the HR system may work remotely up to **3 days**
per week. Fully remote arrangements require VP approval and are reviewed every
**6 months**.

### Home Office Stipend

Remote-eligible employees receive a one-time **$600** home office setup stipend and an
ongoing **$35/month** stipend for internet and utilities, both processed through
ExpenseHawk.

### Core Hours

Regardless of location, all employees are expected to be reachable during core hours of
**10:00 AM to 3:00 PM** in their assigned time zone.

## IT and Security Policy

### Password Requirements

Passwords for internal systems must be at least **14 characters** and rotated every
**180 days**. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all systems handling customer
data.

### Device Encryption

All company-issued laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled before first use. IT
verifies encryption status automatically every **7 days** via the device management
agent.

### VPN Usage

Access to internal tools from outside an office network requires the **NimbusVPN**
client. VPN sessions time out after **12 hours** of inactivity.

### Security Incident Reporting

Suspected security incidents (phishing, lost devices, unauthorized access) must be
reported to `security@nimbuscascade.example` within **1 hour** of discovery. The
security team's target acknowledgment time is **30 minutes**.

## Equipment and Asset Policy

### Laptop Refresh Cycle

Company laptops are refreshed every **3 years**, or sooner if a repair would exceed
**40%** of replacement cost. Requests are submitted through the **AssetDesk** portal.

### Loss or Damage

Accidental damage to company equipment is covered once every **12 months** at no cost to
the employee. A second incident within that window incurs a **$150** deductible.

### Equipment Return

Departing employees must return all company equipment within **5 business days** of
their last day, via the process described in Offboarding.

## Code of Conduct

### Workplace Behavior

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and vendors with respect. Conduct
violations are handled by People & Talent according to a **three-tier** escalation
process: coaching, written warning, and termination.

### Conflict of Interest

Employees must disclose any outside business interest that could conflict with Nimbus
Cascade's business within **30 days** of it arising, using the **Conflict of Interest**
form in LeaveHub.

### Gifts and Entertainment

Gifts from vendors or clients valued over **$75** must be declined or reported to a
manager. Cash gifts of any amount must always be declined.

## Anti-Harassment and Discrimination Policy

### Reporting Process

Reports can be made to a manager, People & Talent, or anonymously through the
**Nimbus Integrity Line**. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is grounds for
immediate termination.

### Investigation Timeline

Investigations begin within **3 business days** of a report and are targeted to
conclude within **30 days**, with interim measures available immediately if needed.

## Performance Reviews

### Review Cycle

Formal performance reviews occur twice a year, in **April** and **October**. Managers
must deliver written feedback at least **5 days** before the review conversation.

### Rating Scale

Performance is rated on a **5-point scale** from "Does Not Meet" to "Significantly
Exceeds." A rating of "Significantly Exceeds" in two consecutive cycles triggers an
automatic promotion review.

### Promotion Process

Promotion requests are submitted by a manager to a calibration committee, which meets
quarterly. Self-nominations are accepted but must include two peer references.

## Compensation and Payroll

### Pay Cycle

Employees are paid **bi-weekly**, on alternating Fridays. Direct deposit is mandatory
after an employee's first **30 days**.

### Bonus Structure

Annual bonus targets range from **8% to 20%** of base salary depending on level, paid
out in **February** based on the prior calendar year's performance rating.

### Salary Review Cycle

Base salaries are reviewed annually in **October**, alongside the performance review
cycle. Off-cycle adjustments require VP and People & Talent approval.

## Benefits and Insurance

### Health Insurance

Nimbus Cascade covers **80%** of health insurance premiums for employees and **50%** for
dependents. Coverage begins on the **first day** of the month following the hire date.

### Dental and Vision

Dental and vision plans are optional add-ons, fully paid by the employee via payroll
deduction, with open enrollment each **November**.

### Retirement Matching

The company matches retirement contributions dollar-for-dollar up to **4%** of base
salary, vesting immediately with no waiting period.

## Onboarding Process

### First Week Checklist

New hires complete IT setup, benefits enrollment, and a security training module within
their first **5 business days**. Equipment is shipped to arrive **2 days** before the
start date.

### Buddy Program

Every new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy from a different team for their first
**60 days**, to help with informal questions outside the reporting line.

### Probation Period

New hires are on a **90-day** probation period, during which either party may end
employment with **1 week's** notice instead of the standard notice period.

## Offboarding Process

### Notice Period

Standard notice period is **2 weeks** for individual contributors and **4 weeks** for
managers and above.

### Exit Interview

People & Talent schedules an exit interview within the employee's final **3 working
days**. Participation is voluntary but strongly encouraged.

### Final Pay and Equipment Return

Final pay, including unused annual leave payout, is issued within **7 days** of the last
working day. Equipment must be returned per the Equipment and Asset Policy.

## Health and Safety Policy

### Workplace Injury Reporting

Any workplace injury, including in a home office, must be reported to People & Talent
within **24 hours** using the **Incident Report** form.

### Ergonomic Assessment

Employees may request a free ergonomic assessment of their home or office workstation
once every **12 months** through Facilities.

## Data Privacy Policy

### Customer Data Handling

Customer data may only be accessed on company-managed devices and must never be
exported to personal storage or personal email. Violations are treated as a security
incident.

### Data Retention

Customer support tickets are retained for **3 years**. Internal HR records are retained
for **7 years** after employment ends, per legal requirement.

## Social Media Policy

### Personal Accounts

Employees may identify their employer on personal social media but must include a
disclaimer that views are their own when discussing industry topics.

### Company Announcements

Only the Marketing team may post on official Nimbus Cascade social accounts. Draft
announcements require Legal review for anything mentioning specific customers.

## Training and Development

### Learning Stipend

Employees receive an annual **$1,000** learning stipend for courses, books, or
certifications, processed through ExpenseHawk like any other reimbursable expense.

### Conference Attendance

One external conference per year is covered beyond the learning stipend, subject to
manager approval and the standard Travel Policy booking process through TripNimbus.

### Certification Reimbursement

Job-relevant certification exam fees are reimbursed in full on **first-attempt pass**;
a second attempt is reimbursed at **50%**.

## Whistleblower Policy

### Reporting Channel

Concerns about legal or ethical violations can be reported through the **Nimbus
Integrity Line**, available 24/7 and operated by an independent third party.

### Non-Retaliation Guarantee

Nimbus Cascade prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith, even if
the underlying concern is not substantiated after investigation.

## Appendix: Key Internal Tools

- **LeaveHub** — leave requests, jury duty, sabbaticals, conflict-of-interest disclosures
- **ExpenseHawk** — expense claims, mileage, learning stipend, home office stipend
- **TripNimbus** — flight and hotel booking
- **AssetDesk** — equipment requests and returns
- **NimbusVPN** — remote access to internal systems
- **Nimbus Integrity Line** — anonymous ethics and harassment reporting
